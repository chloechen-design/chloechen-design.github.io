Context and challenge

Not a brand, a website and a campaign. One recognisable visual world.

Esendeo is a SaaS platform for e-commerce logistics. It lets companies manage, track and centralise deliveries through one interface connected to multiple carriers.

The product had to make a complex B2B operation feel simpler, reliable and scalable. But its visual identity was fragmented, communication varied from one team and channel to another, and every new marketing or sales asset required slow, costly rework.

My responsibility was to clarify the positioning and build one system that could support product growth, daily business use and every customer-facing conversation.

Fragmented identityThe brand changed from one touchpoint to the next.

Complex offerDifferent audiences struggled to identify the right use case.

Slow productionTeams recreated layouts and decisions for every new asset.

Visual driftQuality was difficult to maintain without constant design support.

Brand strategy

Build trust first. Make complexity feel simple.

The strategy connected Esendeo's operational strengths with a more human promise. Reliability and transparency build confidence in a critical logistics service. Speed, simplicity and proactive care make the experience feel useful in everyday work.

We do care.

ReliabilityA system that looks stable enough for high-stakes operations.

TransparencyClear information, visible proof and direct language.

Speed and simplicityFast comprehension and less effort at every step.

ProactivityA partner that anticipates problems instead of reacting late.

Visual foundation

The visual grammar behind every touchpoint.

I translated the strategy into repeatable decisions: a direct typographic voice, a controlled but energetic colour scale, clear hierarchy and a logo system flexible enough for product, partner and sales contexts.

From identity to interface

The website made the system move.

The website had to explain a multi-carrier logistics platform without flattening it into generic SaaS language. I structured the story around user needs, sector-specific entry points and a simple operational sequence: compare, ship, manage and track.

Start from the business contextSolution pages let each company recognise a precise use case before exploring features.

Reduce comprehension effortStrong hierarchy and progressive disclosure turn a technical offer into a readable product story.

Make benefits concreteOperational flows, platform views and customer proof help users understand how Esendeo solves daily logistics problems.

B2B lifecycle communication

Newsletters became part of the service, not an afterthought.

I designed modular structures for onboarding, advanced features, customer feedback and partner announcements. Each message has a precise job, a clear hierarchy and one visible next action, while remaining recognisably Esendeo.

B2B sales system

One visual system, many touchpoints.

The sales deck and print-ready materials turned the same strategy into a reusable commercial narrative. Modular slides help teams present the company, services, transport offer, proof points and client cases clearly without rebuilding every presentation.

Digital to physical

The same system, ready for real conversations.

Sales decks and print materials share one hierarchy, colour logic and tone, while each format keeps its own composition.

End-to-end approach

A system built from strategy, then tested in real use.

The work moved from brand understanding to concrete daily tools, with each stage giving the next one clearer constraints and reusable foundations.

  1. UnderstandUser needs, brand vision and competitive context
  2. DefineValues, positioning, personality and tone of voice
  3. ExploreCreative direction, colour and typography
  4. SystemiseLogo, palette, type, icon and layout rules
  5. ApplyWebsite, newsletters, sales deck and print
  6. EnableGuidelines and reusable assets for product and marketing teams
